2025 - 2026 Research Assistant

Firms' Leverage Targets

Renmin University of China & MIT Sloan collaboration · Working paper

Built a two-stage extraction system combining rule-based retrieval with LLM semantic analysis to identify quantitative leverage targets, motivations, and adjustment behavior from more than 16 GB of earnings-call transcripts and 26,000 SEC 10-K filings.

  • Constructed a new dataset of 9,384 explicit leverage targets across 1,350 firms, covering 43% of total U.S. corporate debt.
  • Developed Lasso and Elastic Net models for out-of-sample target prediction across 473,000 firm-quarter observations.
  • Implemented concurrent API workers, retry logic, checkpoints, and cross-model consistency checks for large-scale processing.

2025 Independent Paper

Geopolitical Risk Perception and Chinese Firms' Dual Response

Innovation response and supply-chain buffering using firm-level data

Measured firm-level geopolitical risk perception from 2016-2024 A-share manufacturing annual reports and studied how perceived exposure shaped R&D and precautionary inventory after the 2021 USICA shock.

  • Created a firm-level text measure from MD&A disclosures using text embeddings and an 84-term localized dictionary.
  • Used a continuous difference-in-differences design with parallel-trend, placebo, exclusion, threshold, and keyword-sensitivity tests.
  • Selected for the 8th National Development Youth Forum and the 12th National Undergraduate Economics Forum.

Strategic Allocation of State Capital

Research report for the National Development and Reform Commission

Designed an industry-classification framework linking strategic policy priorities to national economic sectors, then evaluated state-capital concentration using registration records for approximately 700,000 state-owned enterprises.
